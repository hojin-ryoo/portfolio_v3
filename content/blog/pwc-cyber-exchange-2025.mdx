Proud to have supported PwC's Cyber Exchange 2025. This event brought together top security leaders to address the most pressing issues facing CISOs today.

My contributions included creating agentic workflows and context-driven code generation using real cyber incident data, and managing the cloud infrastructure that hosted our solutions. I also had the pleasure of facilitating a session on rapid AI website prototyping using v0.

The discussions underscored the strategic importance of the CISO role in the c-suite, especially as we navigate rapidly changing technological, demographic, political, and threat landscapes. It was rewarding to see how our technical solutions helped facilitate these important conversations.
